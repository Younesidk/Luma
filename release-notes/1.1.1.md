## Luma 1.1.1

### New: start with Windows

- Luma can now start by itself when you sign in to Windows and bring back your live wallpaper, so you don't have to open it after every restart
- It starts quietly in the taskbar's notification area without opening its window. Click the Luma icon there whenever you want the window
- Turn it on in Settings → Live wallpapers → "Start with Windows" (it's off until you switch it on)
- You can also turn it off from Task Manager's Startup apps, and Luma's switch follows along

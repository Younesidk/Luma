## Luma 1.1.0

### New: live wallpapers

- Turn any MP4, MOV or WebM video into a moving wallpaper behind your desktop icons (Windows 10 and 11)
- Find them on the new Live page: import a video, preview it, then set it on every display or just one (Fill, Fit or Stretch)
- The video plays muted and pauses by itself when you can't see it: when the desktop is covered, during fullscreen games and apps, when the PC is locked, asleep or the screen is off, and (optional) on battery
- Your live wallpaper comes back when Luma starts, and its still frame stays on the desktop whenever the video isn't playing
- Videos much larger than your screen (for example 4K on a 1080p display) are resized to fit when you import them. They look the same but use far less memory. Your original file is never changed
- A paused video frees its memory after 20 seconds and picks up where it left off when it plays again

### Luma now keeps running in the background

- Closing the window keeps Luma running in the taskbar's notification area (the small arrow next to the clock), so your live wallpaper and rotation keep going
- Click the Luma icon there to open the window again. Right-click it to pause the live wallpaper or to quit Luma
- Opening Luma from the Start menu while it's already running brings back the existing window
- Prefer the old behaviour? Turn off Settings → Live wallpapers → "Keep running in the background when closed"
